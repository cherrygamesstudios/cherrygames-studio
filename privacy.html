Privacy Policy – Boss Fight Fantasy

Developer: Cherry Games Studio

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Android game Boss Fight Fantasy, published on Google Play by Cherry Games Studio (Michał Wrześniewski).

Information we collect

Boss Fight Fantasy does not require users to create an account. The developer does not directly request personal information such as a user's name, postal address, phone number, or payment information.

The game uses third-party services, including Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display advertisements. These services may automatically collect or process information such as device or other identifiers (including the Advertising ID where available), approximate location derived from technical information, app interactions, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Advertising

Boss Fight Fantasy contains advertisements provided through Google AdMob. Depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Google and its advertising partners may process device information and identifiers to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Third-party services

The game uses Google services, including Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms. More information is available at Google's partner technologies page.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the app. Data handled by third-party services is transmitted and processed according to the security practices of those providers.

Children

Boss Fight Fantasy does not provide its own account-registration system and is not designed to directly request personal information from children. Third-party services are used subject to applicable Google Play policies and legal requirements.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
